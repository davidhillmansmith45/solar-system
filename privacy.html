Privacy Policy for Solar System Explorer

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Solar System Explorer (“the App”), published by David Smith (“we,” “us”), handles information.

1. Overview

Solar System Explorer is an educational app that shows an interactive model of the solar system. The App does not require an account and does not include ads.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information through the App.

The App does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or social logins.

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or files.

3. How the App works on your device

The App runs on your device. Simulation settings and similar preferences, if any, stay on your device and are not sent to us.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Third-party services

The App does not embed third-party tracking or advertising services. Google Play may collect information as described in Google’s own policies when you download or use apps from Google Play.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
